Chinook Music Store

Employee Handbook

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 January 2026

Fictional handbook created for the Gen_AI learning project. All policies are made up for practice.

1. Welcome to Chinook

Chinook is an online music store that sells individual tracks, full albums and music videos to customers in 24 countries. The company was founded in 2009 in Calgary, Canada, where the head office is still located at 825 8 Ave SW. Our catalogue contains more than 3,500 tracks from 275 artists across 25 genres, with Rock, Latin and Metal being the three largest genres by number of tracks.

Our mission is simple: make it easy for anyone to find, buy and enjoy music they love. Every employee, whether in sales support, IT or management, is expected to put the customer experience first. This handbook explains how we work, what customers can expect from us, and what we expect from you.

The handbook is reviewed every year by the General Manager. If anything in it is unclear, ask your manager or email hr@chinookcorp.com. In case of a conflict between this handbook and your employment contract, the contract always takes priority.

2. Products and Pricing

2.1 What we sell

We sell three kinds of products: audio tracks, music videos and complete albums. Audio tracks are delivered in either MPEG audio (MP3) or AAC format; customers choose the format at checkout and can switch formats for free within 30 days. Music videos are delivered as protected MPEG-4 video files.

2.2 Standard prices

ProductPrice (USD)
Standard audio track$0.99
Music video or TV episode$1.99
Album (any length)$9.99, or the sum of its tracks if that is lower

Prices are shown in US dollars on every invoice. Customers outside the United States pay the converted amount on the day of purchase; Chinook does not charge any currency conversion fee, although the customer's bank might. Sales tax or VAT is added at checkout where required by law.

2.3 Promotions

Seasonal promotions are approved by the Sales Manager. A promotion can never reduce a track below $0.49. Promotion codes are single-use and expire 60 days after they are issued. Sales Support Agents may not create promotion codes themselves; they must request them from the Sales Manager.

3. Payments, Refunds and Gift Cards

3.1 Payment methods

Customers can pay by credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit card or PayPal. We do not accept cash, cheques or cryptocurrency. Employees must never ask a customer for their full card number by email, chat or phone; all payments go through the secure checkout page only.

3.2 Refund policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the track or video has been downloaded no more than twice. After 14 days, refunds are only given when the file is faulty, for example when it will not play or is clearly cut short. Refunds are always returned to the original payment method and are processed within 5 business days of approval.

Sales Support Agents can approve refunds up to $20 on their own. Refunds above $20 must be approved by the Sales Manager. A customer may receive at most three refunds in any 12-month period; further requests must be escalated.

3.3 Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in values of $10, $25 and $50. Gift cards never expire and cannot be exchanged for cash. A lost gift card can only be replaced if the customer still has the original purchase receipt or code email.

4. Customer Accounts and Playlists

Every customer has one account linked to one email address. Customers can create up to 50 playlists, and each playlist can contain up to 500 tracks. Playlists can be private or shared by link. The store also publishes curated playlists such as "Grunge", "Classical" and "90's Music", which customers can copy but not edit.

Each customer is assigned a personal Sales Support Agent when the account is created. The agent is the customer's first point of contact for questions about purchases, refunds and account problems. Assignments are balanced so that each agent looks after roughly the same number of customers.

If a customer forgets their password, they must use the "Reset password" link on the login page. For security reasons, employees can never see, set or send a customer's password. If the customer no longer has access to their email address, the request must be escalated to the IT Manager, who will verify the customer's identity using their last two invoice numbers.

5. Customer Support

5.1 Opening hours

Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 Mountain Time, by email and live chat. Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Mountain Time. There is no support on weekends or on Canadian public holidays, but customers can still buy music 24 hours a day.

5.2 Response times

Every email must receive a first reply within one business day. Live chat requests should be answered within 2 minutes during opening hours. Complaints about billing errors are high priority and must be resolved within 3 business days.

5.3 Escalation

Problems are escalated in this order: first the customer's Sales Support Agent, then the Sales Manager, and finally the General Manager. Technical problems such as website errors or failed downloads go directly to the IT Manager. Always tell the customer who is handling their case and when they will hear back.

5.4 Tone of voice

Be friendly, clear and honest. Use the customer's name, avoid technical jargon, and never promise something the policy does not allow. If you do not know the answer, say so and find out, rather than guessing.

6. Our Team

Chinook has eight employees organised in three teams. The General Manager leads the company and directly manages the Sales Manager and the IT Manager.

TeamRoles
ManagementGeneral Manager

Sales Support Agents report to the Sales Manager. IT Staff report to the IT Manager. New employees are paired with an experienced colleague for their first four weeks.

7. Pay, Commission and Benefits

7.1 Commission

Sales Support Agents earn a commission of 5% on the total value of all invoices from the customers assigned to them, paid monthly together with salary. Commission is calculated on the invoice total after refunds. The Sales Manager earns a team bonus of 2% on all sales invoices. IT staff do not earn commission but receive an annual performance bonus.

7.2 Working hours and leave

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Employees can work from home up to two days per week with their manager's approval. Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, plus Canadian public holidays. Vacation requests should be made at least two weeks in advance. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year.

7.3 Employee discount

All employees receive a 20% discount on tracks, albums and videos for personal use. The discount cannot be combined with promotion codes and may not be used to buy music for resale.

8. Data Protection and Security

Customer data such as names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and invoices must only be used to serve that customer. Never share customer data with other customers, copy it to personal devices, or discuss it outside work.

All employees must use a password of at least 12 characters and turn on two-factor authentication for their work account. Passwords must never be shared, including with colleagues or managers. Report any suspicious email, lost laptop or possible data leak to the IT Manager immediately, even outside working hours.

Customer invoices are kept for 7 years for tax purposes. When a customer closes their account, their personal details are deleted within 30 days, except for the invoice records that the law requires us to keep.
